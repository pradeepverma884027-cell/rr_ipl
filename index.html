How the pressure logic works

Chase innings

Required run rate measures urgency. Lost wickets and innings progress add resource stress.

100 × [0.55·clip((RRR−7)/6,0,1)
+ 0.25·(wickets lost/10)
+ 0.20·(1−balls left/120)]
First innings

Without a required rate, use current scoring pace, wickets lost and innings progress.

100 × [0.50·(1−balls left/120)
+ 0.30·(wickets lost/10)
+ 0.20·clip((CRR−7)/6,0,1)]
Eligible sample

Pressure is measured immediately before each delivery. Death overs alone do not guarantee a high-pressure label.

High pressure: score ≥ 60
Batter minimum: 30 pressure + 30 baseline balls; 5 matches
Bowler minimum: 60 pressure + 60 baseline legal balls; 5 matches

Performance rating

Batters

raw = 50 + 0.35·(pressure SR−140)
+ 0.35·(pressure SR−normal SR)
final = reliability·raw + (1−reliability)·50
reliability = pressure balls/(pressure balls+100)

Bowlers

raw = 50 + 0.4·(8.5−pressure econ)
+ 0.25·(normal econ−pressure econ)
+ 0.3·(pressure wickets/pressure overs·10)
final shrinks toward 50; k=180 balls

Ratings are heuristic comparative indices, not probabilities. Baselines pool IPL and T20I records; venue, opponent, role and revised-target timing are limitations.

Eligibility filters

Player identity is joined by player ID. Pakistan and Bangladesh national-team representatives are excluded using supplied T20I team mappings. Players on the official IPL 2027 retained lists of CSK, DC, GT, KKR, LSG, MI, PBKS, RCB and SRH are excluded. RR-retained players remain eligible for statistical rankings. RR-retained players are not auction targets.

The RR auction shortlist is empty until auction registration and player availability can be verified. Released status alone is not treated as proof of availability.
